What is in the bag

The one book transfer that was saved

Store B already held the book from edition 1, so the amended edition was sent as two blocks. The third bar is what a full resend would have weighed; it was written only to measure, never sent.

How the bag crossed between two stores

Store A (island, Node, flat files) and Store B (mainland, Python, SQLite) share no directory, no code and no process (they sit on the same Mac, in different folders). Only three files crossed. Step through it.

Check it yourself, in this browser

This page recomputes SHA-256 with your browser's own crypto and compares it with the digest inside each CID. Nothing is uploaded; the book is read from your disk only if you choose it.

    Exact format and credits

    Credits

    The book is the pocket astronomy EPUB Four moons of Jupiter published in Lakeshore-Labs/computer, commit . It carries the NASA/JPL/DLR photograph PIA00601, JPL's planetary satellite table and Wikidata entities.

    CAR and CID tooling: @ipld/car, multiformats, @ipld/dag-cbor, blockstore-fs (writer, Store A); carbox and libipld (independent reader, Store B). Specs: CARv1, CID, DAG-CBOR. Full list with versions and licenses: SOURCES.md.

    No public IPFS node, gateway, pinning service or swarm was contacted: the CAR files are ordinary local files, and this page makes no network requests. Read README.md for the replay commands and what was not done.
