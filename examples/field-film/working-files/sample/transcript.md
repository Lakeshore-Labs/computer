# Where the files go

## The museum counter

A visitor asks for a postcard of Irises. The browser writes the order into a Computer and runs the print recipe.

Change the painting and the inscription. The browser edits the order and downloads the new card from that same desk.

## The print queue

The first proof has three copies of Irises. The museum changes its order before printing.

Computer lays out one Getty card and three Cleveland cards. The amended PDF reaches a virtual print queue, byte for byte.

## The learner’s deck

An astronomy student studies Europa. Computer publishes an amended four-moon study deck.

Anki updates one note and keeps the learner’s card identity, schedule and first review. The other three notes stay as they were.

## The radio booth

A Computer makes a short station cue and stores it through NATS. The server restarts before the subscriber arrives.

The subscriber recovers and acknowledges the cue. This is the sound from that recovered file.

[Recovered station cue]

## The lunch round

Three kitchens have enough meals altogether, but the portions are in the wrong places.

Computer joins the orders to the loaded crates through two database servers. Four transfers balance all one hundred and fifty portions.

## The island book club

A book club changes its meeting time. The island already has its copy of the four-moon book.

Computer sends the revised reading card in just over a kilobyte. The receiver reuses the original book and opens the new invitation.
