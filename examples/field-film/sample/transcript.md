# Computer field guide

## The ferry ledger

A ferry timetable arrives through an MCP server. Computer reads fifty thousand rows and writes a SQLite database.

The shell returns the route totals in just one hundred and forty-six bytes of text. A separate read brings back this chart.

## Windows over Venice

Over Venice, four byte-range requests bring three windows of a satellite image into Computer.

An independent reader matches every pixel. The full image is over a hundred megabytes.

## A designer’s palette

A Swift client uses Computer to turn the same satellite pixels into six colors a designer can keep.

The palette opens in native Mac software, and the colors also export to Adobe, GIMP, and CSS.

## Oslofjord in relief

Near Oslo, Computer turns elevation samples into two solid terrain models.

A separate slicer checks both. The taller relief takes eighty-four layers of material.

## Sunday’s fitting

The wardrobe has five jackets for seven ushers. The assistant finds three more and saves the count offline.

Back online, Computer reads the browser’s files and fits everyone. One jacket stays on the rack.

## Last Boat Home

Two Computer shells turn one tune into flute and clarinet parts. Their written pitches differ, but their sounding notes agree.
