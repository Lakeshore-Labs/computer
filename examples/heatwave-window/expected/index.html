Morning and afternoon, 19 July 2022: modelled air temperature over southern Britain

Each coloured block is one ~0.25° cell of the ERA5 reanalysis grid (about 28 km north–south), showing the model's 2 m air temperature at one instant. Compare 06:00 UTC (07:00 British Summer Time) with 15:00 UTC (16:00 BST), and the change between them. Select a block, or use the arrow keys.

Read this as a model grid, not a weather station. A cell value is a spatial estimate for a ~28 km box that blends observations with a physics model. It is not a reading from a thermometer at the named town, and a town marker is only where the town lies inside its cell.

The three named grid locations

City coordinates are approximate public city-centre values rounded to 0.01°, entered by hand (not fetched). “Cell” is the nearest ERA5 grid point to them, at 0.25° spacing.

What this does not show

  • Not station thermometer records, and no station comparison is made.
  • One day, two instants; not a trend, climate statement or ranking.
  • Cells over the sea and coast are model cells too. No coastline is drawn; none was available from a tiny public-domain source here.
  • Colours are scaled to this window only. The change map is centred on zero (blue = cooler at 15:00 than 06:00, purple = warmer).
